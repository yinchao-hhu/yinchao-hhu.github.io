个人简介 Biography

我于 2019 年本科毕业于北京理工大学数学与统计学院，随后在南京大学数学系直博，2024 年获博士学位，导师为杨俊锋教授。

I received my bachelor's degree from the School of Mathematics and Statistics at Beijing Institute of Technology in 2019. I then entered the direct PhD program at the Department of Mathematics, Nanjing University, where I completed my PhD in 2024 under Prof. Junfeng Yang.

研究方向 Research interests

我的研究方向是分布式优化、联邦学习、双层规划和一阶算法。近期工作主要涉及去中心化双层优化、自适应步长、原始-对偶分裂和 ADMM 类方法，关注通信受限和数据异质条件下的算法设计与收敛性。

I work on distributed optimization, federated learning, bilevel optimization, and first-order methods. My recent work includes decentralized bilevel optimization, adaptive stepsizes, primal-dual splitting, and ADMM-type methods. I study how communication constraints and data heterogeneity affect algorithm design and convergence.

学生招生 Prospective students

Feel free to email me if you are interested in optimization algorithms and theory, or in training and using large language models. I look for a solid math background, the ability to read research papers, and proficiency in Python and LaTeX. I also encourage students to use tools such as Codex and Claude Code for research and programming.
