About

个人简介 Biography

中文简介

尹超，河海大学数学学院讲师。2019 年本科毕业于北京理工大学数学与统计学院，2024 年直博毕业于南京大学数学系，导师为杨俊锋教授。 研究兴趣包括分布式优化、联邦学习、双层规划以及一阶算法理论。 近期工作围绕去中心化分布式优化、双层优化、自适应步长与原始-对偶算法，关注通信受限、数据异质和大规模机器学习场景下的收敛性分析与高效算法设计。 主持国家自然科学基金青年项目和江苏省自然科学基金青年项目。

English Bio

Chao Yin is a lecturer at the School of Mathematics, Hohai University. He received his B.S. degree from the School of Mathematics and Statistics, Beijing Institute of Technology in 2019, and his Ph.D. degree from the Department of Mathematics, Nanjing University in 2024, under the supervision of Prof. Junfeng Yang. His research focuses on distributed optimization, federated learning, first-order methods, and bilevel optimization. His recent work studies decentralized bilevel optimization, adaptive stepsizes, primal-dual splitting, ADMM-type methods, and convergence theory under communication constraints and data heterogeneity. He leads youth projects funded by the National Natural Science Foundation of China and the Natural Science Foundation of Jiangsu Province.

研究方向 Research Interests

一阶算法 First-Order Methods

一阶优化算法、收敛性分析、复杂度界和参数选择机制。

First-order optimization algorithms, convergence analysis, complexity bounds, and parameter selection.

分布式优化 Distributed Optimization

去中心化算法、梯度跟踪、通信约束与网络结构影响。

Decentralized algorithms, gradient tracking, communication limits, and graph effects.

联邦学习 Federated Learning

自适应方法、异质数据、客户端采样和鲁棒训练。

Adaptive methods, data heterogeneity, client sampling, and robust training.

双层规划 Bilevel Optimization

双层优化、超参数优化、数据清洗和元学习中的算法设计与收敛分析。

Algorithm design and convergence analysis for bilevel optimization, hyperparameter optimization, data cleaning, and meta-learning.
